# Fazal-e-Rashid Vision — Website Build Spec

## Company Overview
Fazal-e-Rashid Vision is a computer vision and digitization company. We design and deploy AI-powered camera systems and business digitization solutions for industrial clients, offices, and homes.

## Services to List on the Site

1. **Fraud-Prevention Counting Systems**
   Camera-based computer vision installed on conveyor belts / loading points that automatically counts pieces in real time, eliminating fraud and errors from manual counting during truck loading.

2. **Smart Security & Surveillance**
   App-connected security cameras for homes, farmhouses, offices, and second properties. Uses recognition to flag unfamiliar/unrecognized individuals on the premises — not a standard attendance or generic security system, but targeted intrusion/anomaly detection.

3. **AI-Integrated Facility Monitoring**
   Full-building camera networks with facial recognition and no blind spots. Includes:
   - Audio recording, transcription, and summarization of on-site conversations
   - Automated employee attendance tracking
   - Twice-daily (day and night) activity summary reports
   - Multi-building management through a single unified app

4. **Business Digitization & Automation**
   Helping companies go paperless — digitizing records and workflows, and building custom chatbots for internal or customer-facing use.
