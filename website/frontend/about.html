Who We Are

The Vision Behind
the Company

Fazal-e-Rashid Vision is a family-founded company built around two disciplines that rarely come together: computer vision and business digitization. We don't just sell cameras — we engineer accountability systems.

The idea came from a direct observation of the losses that Pakistani businesses quietly absorb every day: stock that leaves a warehouse without being counted, unauthorized individuals entering properties unchecked, employees whose hours are unverifiable, and records that live in filing cabinets nobody reads. These are solvable problems — and AI gives us the tools to solve them reliably.

Our co-founders — Khalil and Shahmir — bring together experience in technology, operations, and client-facing consulting. Being a family-run business means decisions are made quickly, commitments are personal, and every deployment gets the attention it deserves.

We serve industrial clients, office buildings, and private property owners — any environment where what's happening on-site needs to be captured accurately and acted on immediately.
