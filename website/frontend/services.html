Our Solutions

What We Build for You

Four precision-engineered solutions that bring AI-driven accountability, security, and efficiency to your operations — from the factory floor to the front office.

Service 01

Fraud-Prevention Counting Systems

Manual counting during truck loading is one of the most exploited gaps in industrial supply chains. A count error — accidental or deliberate — can cost thousands per shift. Our camera-based computer vision system installs directly on your conveyor belts or loading points and counts every piece in real time, logging the data automatically with full traceability.

The result: a tamper-proof record of every item that leaves your facility. Discrepancies are flagged instantly, giving supervisors the evidence they need and removing the temptation entirely.

  • Real-time piece-by-piece counting on conveyor belts and loading points
  • Automated logging with timestamped, tamper-proof records
  • Instant discrepancy alerts sent to management
  • Compatible with existing conveyor and loading infrastructure
  • Scalable across multiple loading bays simultaneously
🔒
Service 02

Smart Security & Surveillance

Standard CCTV records footage nobody watches until after something goes wrong. Our smart surveillance system is fundamentally different — it uses recognition intelligence to actively distinguish between people you know and people you don't.

App-connected cameras for your home, farmhouse, office, or second property learn who belongs on-site and immediately flag any unfamiliar or unrecognized individual, delivering a real-time alert to your phone before an incident escalates.

  • Targeted intrusion and anomaly detection — not generic CCTV
  • Recognition-based alerts: familiar vs. unrecognized individuals
  • App-connected for real-time notifications on your phone
  • Suitable for homes, farmhouses, offices, and vacation properties
  • Works around the clock — day and night
🏢
Service 03

AI-Integrated Facility Monitoring

Designed for multi-story offices, industrial sites, or any facility where accountability and operational oversight are critical. We design and deploy a full-building camera network with no blind spots, anchored by facial recognition and connected to a unified management dashboard.

The system goes beyond video — on-site conversations are captured, transcribed, and summarized automatically, employee attendance is tracked without check-in devices, and you receive structured activity reports twice daily (morning and evening). Multiple buildings or campuses can be managed from a single app.

  • Full-building camera coverage with zero blind spots
  • Facial recognition for access tracking and identity verification
  • Audio recording, AI transcription, and conversation summarization
  • Automated employee attendance — no extra hardware required
  • Day and night activity summary reports delivered automatically
  • Multi-building management through one unified application

Service 04

Business Digitization & Automation

Paper-based workflows are slow, error-prone, and impossible to audit at scale. We help businesses transition entirely to digital — converting physical records into structured, searchable systems and automating repetitive processes that consume staff time.

Beyond record-keeping, we build custom AI chatbots tailored to your operations — whether that's an internal assistant for your team or a customer-facing tool that handles queries, requests, and routing without manual intervention.

  • End-to-end paperless conversion of records and workflows
  • Structured digital archives with easy search and retrieval
  • Process automation to reduce manual overhead
  • Custom internal chatbots for staff queries and task management
  • Customer-facing chatbots for routing, FAQs, and support
